Here are the most meaningful, high-impact features you can add to elevate the platform from an event management tool into a **complete alumni experience ecosystem**:

---

### 1. 🤝 Alumni Networking & "Who's Coming" Directory
* **"Find My Batchmates" Directory**: A searchable list where registered alumni can see who from their batch, branch, or current city is attending (with privacy toggles to hide phone/email).
* **Company & City Filters**: Allows alumni to discover peers working in the same city, industry, or company for post-event local meetups and professional networking.

---

### 2. 🍽️ Digital Food & Beverage Coupon Redemption
* **Anti-Fraud Meal Tokens**: Add individual digital punch tokens (e.g., *Gala Dinner*, *High Tea*, *Mementos*) directly on the ID Card pass.
* **Catering Counter Scanner**: Food counter volunteers can scan the badge QR code to mark meals as *Redeemed* in real-time, preventing duplicate food servings.

---

### 3. 📸 Live Event Memory Wall & Photo Stream
* **Crowdsourced Photo Uploads**: Allow verified attendees to upload photos and selfies taken during the reunion directly from their mobile pass.
* **Auditorium Projector Slideshow**: A live, auto-refreshing photo stream designed to be projected on big screens on the college grounds during the gala evening.

---

### 4. 📳 Automated WhatsApp Notifications (via API)
* **Direct Event Pings**: Move beyond manual `wa.me` links to automated delivery via WhatsApp Business API (e.g., Twilio, Aisensy, or Gupshup).
* **Automated Trigger Milestones**:
  - Instant payment confirmation & pass download link upon admin approval.
  - T-24 Hour reminder with venue map location and entry QR pass.
  - "Welcome to ECK Campus!" message upon gate check-in with the evening agenda.

---

### 5. 🚗 Travel & Accommodation Coordination Board
* **Carpool & Ride Sharing**: Alumni traveling from Jaipur, Delhi, or Indore to Kota can post available car seats or coordinate shared taxis.
* **Hotel & Guest House Guide**: Partner hotel discounts and nearby accommodation booking contact points curated for outstation alumni.

---

### 6. 📱 Progressive Web App (PWA) & Offline Gate Check-in
* **Installable Gate App**: Allow gate volunteers to add the Gate Portal to their phone home screen like a native mobile app.
* **Offline-First Check-In**: Cache registration records locally on gate volunteers' devices so check-in continues uninterrupted even if the campus ground loses cellular connectivity, syncing back automatically when reconnected.

---

### 7. 🎓 Batch Contribution & Campus Legacy Fund
* **"Batch Gift" Add-on**: Let alumni add optional donations (e.g., ₹500, ₹1000, ₹5000) during registration toward college labs, student scholarships, or alumni library initiatives.
* **Batch Leaderboard**: Display a live ticker of total funds contributed by each graduating batch to spark healthy, friendly nostalgia-driven competition.

---

### 8. 📜 Digital Certificate of Reunion & Lifetime Memento
* **Downloadable Certificate of Attendance**: Post-event, verified attendees receive an officially styled e-certificate acknowledging their participation in DeepAura.
* **LinkedIn Share Integration**: One-click sharing of their participation certificate to LinkedIn, boosting college alumni brand visibility.

---

### 9. 📢 Live Stage Timeline & Real-Time Announcements
* **Dynamic Agenda View**: A live schedule timeline on the attendee pass (e.g., *6:30 PM: Welcome Address*, *8:00 PM: Batch Group Photos*, *9:00 PM: Gala Dinner*).
* **Emergency / Announcement Banner**: Admin broadcast banner that instantly pops up on attendees' mobile pass screens (e.g., *"Batch of 2012 assemble at Main Stage for group photo"*).
